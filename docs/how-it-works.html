How the reunification engine works

The reunification journey

From a panicked report to a family reunited — usually in seconds.

1

🎙️ Report रिपोर्ट दें

A family describes the missing person — by voice in any of 9 languages, by tapping big icons (gender, age, last-seen place), or by snapping a photo. No login, huge buttons, the app talks back. Or a pilgrim taps "I am lost" and is guided to the nearest help desk.

Pilgrim PWA9 languagesvoice · photo · taps
2

🧠 Understand समझना

Sarvam STT transcribes the speech in native script. Claude normalises the messy, code-mixed report into one canonical-English profile (age band, gender, clothing, language, landmark) — and Claude Vision reads the photo into the same vocabulary, so a picture can match a text record.

Sarvam SaarikaClaude extractionClaude Vision (VLM)
3

🧩 Match मिलान

The engine scores the report against everyone logged "found" across every centre — face + attribute + geo, with a hard gender veto and weight redistribution for missing data. Claude re-ranks the top candidates and writes a one-line "why it matched" a volunteer can act on.

face + attribute + georanked top-Kexplained by Claude
4

🤖 Sahayak dispatches सहायक

An agentic dispatcher works the case like a control-room officer: it predicts where the person drifted using real Kumbh crowd geography (ghats, chokepoints), dispatches a localised loudspeaker announcement to the right help desks, and re-checks as new "found" reports arrive.

drift-zone predictioncrowd chokepointsauto announce
5

🪔 Reunite पुनर्मिलन

On a strong match the family is called and brought together. Children are never publicly announced — a minor triggers a private staff alert and guardian verification instead (child-safety §12). Every confirm and announce is audit-logged; no names or phones ever appear in logs.

The trick no one else has

Two ideas that make SETU more than a search box.

🖼️ Cross-modal matching — photo ↔ text

The provided dataset has no photos, so face-recognition can't match a family's snapshot against text-only records. Claude Vision instead converts the photo into the same canonical attributes the records use — so a photo of grandpa matches a text-logged "elderly man, blue kurta, walking stick."

📷 photo→Claude Vision→elderly · male · blue kurta · stick→🧩 match

🤖 Sahayak — the agentic reunification dispatcher

It doesn't wait. For each open case it reasons step-by-step and you watch it live on the map.

① read→② scan every centre→③ predict drift zone→④ dispatch announcement→⑤ notify family
